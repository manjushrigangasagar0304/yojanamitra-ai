# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

Ministry: Ministry of Finance (small savings scheme), part of Beti Bachao Beti Padhao
Type: Central government savings scheme, launched 2015
Where: India Post and authorised banks

## Objective
Encourage parents to save for a girl child's education and marriage.

## Benefits
- Interest rate 8.2% per year for October-December 2026 (rate is reviewed every quarter), compounded yearly.
- Minimum deposit Rs 250 a year, maximum Rs 1.5 lakh a year.
- Deposits are made for 15 years; the account matures 21 years after opening.
- Deposits qualify for deduction under Section 80C (old tax regime); interest and maturity amount are tax-free.
- Up to 50% of the balance can be withdrawn after the girl turns 18, for higher education.

## Eligibility
- Account is opened in the name of a girl child below 10 years of age by a parent or legal guardian.
- Girl must be an Indian resident.
- Maximum one account per girl and two accounts per family (three if twins or triplets are born in the second birth).

## Documents required
- Birth certificate of the girl child
- Aadhaar and PAN of the parent/guardian
- Address proof of the parent/guardian
- Passport-size photographs

## How to apply
1. Visit any post office or an authorised bank branch (SBI, Bank of Baroda, PNB, ICICI, HDFC and others).
2. Fill in the SSY account opening form and submit documents.
3. Make the first deposit (minimum Rs 250).
4. Deposit every year to keep the account active; a default penalty of Rs 50 a year applies otherwise.
