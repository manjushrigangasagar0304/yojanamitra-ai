# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

Ministry: Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas
Type: Central scheme, launched May 2016; current phase Ujjwala 2.0
Official: https://www.pmuy.gov.in

## Objective
Give clean cooking fuel (LPG) to women from poor households, reducing indoor smoke and health risks from wood and cow-dung fuel.

## Benefits
- Security-deposit-free LPG connection in the woman's name, with no installation charges.
- The connection covers the cylinder (14.2 kg or 5 kg), pressure regulator, Suraksha hose and DGCC booklet. Government support is about Rs 1,600 for a 14.2 kg connection.
- Under Ujjwala 2.0, the first refill and a stove (hotplate) are free.
- Targeted subsidy of Rs 300 per 14.2 kg cylinder for up to 9 refills a year (FY 2025-26 rate; check the current year), so up to Rs 2,700 a year.

## Eligibility
- Applicant must be a woman aged 18 or above.
- She must belong to a poor household. Since the expansion, eligibility is shown through a Deprivation Declaration in the standard format.
- Households from SC/ST, PMAY-G beneficiaries, Antyodaya Anna Yojana, forest dwellers, tea garden tribes, island residents and similar categories are covered.
- There must be no existing LPG connection from any oil marketing company in the same household.

## Documents required
- Aadhaar card of the applicant
- Ration card or other document showing family composition
- Bank account details (IFSC and account number) for the subsidy
- Passport-size photograph
- Deprivation Declaration (standard form, for poor households)

## How to apply
1. Apply online at pmuy.gov.in and choose an oil company (Indane, Bharat Gas or HP Gas) and distributor, or visit the nearest LPG distributor.
2. Submit the KYC form with documents.
3. The distributor verifies eligibility, then releases the connection.

## Helpline
