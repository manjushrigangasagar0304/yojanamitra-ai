# Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY)

Ministry: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare; implemented by the National Health Authority (NHA)
Type: Centrally sponsored health assurance scheme, launched September 2018
Official: https://nha.gov.in/PM-JAY | https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in

## Objective
Protect poor and vulnerable families, and all senior citizens aged 70+, from catastrophic hospital expenses.

## Benefits
- Cashless and paperless treatment up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year at empanelled public and private hospitals.
- Covers secondary and tertiary hospitalisation, including pre-hospitalisation and post-hospitalisation expenses.
- No cap on family size or age of members.
- Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one.
- This is health cover, not a cash payment.

## Eligibility
Route 1: Senior citizens (Ayushman Vay Vandana card)
- All Indian citizens aged 70 years or above, irrespective of income or socio-economic status.
- Seniors outside existing PM-JAY families get cover up to Rs 5 lakh per year on a family basis.
- Seniors in families already covered get an extra top-up of up to Rs 5 lakh per year for themselves only.
- Seniors with private insurance or ESI are also eligible.

Route 2: Poor and vulnerable families
- Families identified from the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 deprivation criteria in rural areas and occupational criteria in urban areas, plus families added by states.
- There is no income-based self-registration. A family must check whether its name is on the list.

## Documents required
- Aadhaar card (Aadhaar-based eKYC is mandatory)
- Ration card or PM letter / family ID for Route 2
- Mobile number

## How to apply
1. Check eligibility on beneficiary.nha.gov.in or the Ayushman app using a mobile number and Aadhaar.
2. If eligible, complete Aadhaar eKYC and download the Ayushman card.
3. Seniors aged 70+ can self-enrol on the same portal to get the Ayushman Vay Vandana card.
4. At an empanelled hospital, the Ayushman Mitra desk helps with verification and cashless treatment.

## Helpline
National helpline 14555.
