# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India
Type: Central Sector Scheme, launched February 2019
Official: https://pmkisan.gov.in | https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pm-kisan

## Objective
Income support to landholding farmer families so they can meet expenses on farm inputs and household needs.

## Benefits
- Rs 6,000 per year per farmer family.
- Paid in three equal instalments of Rs 2,000 every four months.
- Money is sent by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the Aadhaar-seeded bank account.

## Eligibility
- All landholding farmer families that have cultivable land recorded in their names.
- A "family" means husband, wife and minor children.
- Both small and large landholders are covered, subject to the exclusions below.
- eKYC (Aadhaar OTP or biometric) is mandatory to keep receiving instalments.

## Exclusions
- Institutional landholders.
- Families where any member held a constitutional post, or is a former/serving minister, MP, MLA, mayor or district panchayat chairperson.
- Serving or retired officers and employees of central/state governments and PSUs (Multi Tasking Staff / Class IV / Group D employees are not excluded).
- Retired pensioners getting Rs 10,000 or more per month.
- Anyone who paid income tax in the last assessment year.
- Registered professionals: doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants, architects.

## Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Land ownership records (in Maharashtra: 7/12 extract and 8A)
- Aadhaar-linked bank account details
- Mobile number for eKYC

## How to apply
1. Visit pmkisan.gov.in and click "New Farmer Registration" under Farmers Corner.
2. Enter Aadhaar number, mobile number and state; complete OTP verification.
3. Fill in land details and bank details, and submit.
4. The state government verifies land records, then the farmer starts receiving instalments.
5. Alternatively, register at the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or through the village patwari / agriculture officer.

## Helpline
PM-KISAN helpline 155261 / 011-24300606. Status can be checked under "Know Your Status" on the portal.
