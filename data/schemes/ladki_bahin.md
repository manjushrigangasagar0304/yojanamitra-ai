# Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana (Maharashtra)

Department: Women and Child Development Department, Government of Maharashtra
Type: State scheme, launched July 2024
Official: https://ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in | https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/mmlby

## Objective
Improve the economic independence, health and nutrition of women from low-income families in Maharashtra.

## Benefits
- Rs 1,500 per month (Rs 18,000 a year) paid by DBT into the woman's Aadhaar-linked bank account.
- Women already receiving money under another state scheme get only the difference up to Rs 1,500 (for example, Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi beneficiaries get Rs 500 under this scheme).

## Eligibility
- Applicant must be a woman and a permanent resident (domicile) of Maharashtra.
- Age between 21 and 65 years.
- Married, widowed, divorced, abandoned, destitute women, and one unmarried woman per family, are eligible.
- Annual family income must not exceed Rs 2.5 lakh.
- Must have a bank account in her own name linked to Aadhaar.

## Exclusions
- Families where any member is an income-tax payer.
- Families where any member is a permanent government employee or a government pensioner.
- Families owning a four-wheeler (excluding tractors).
- Women already receiving Rs 1,500 or more from another government scheme.

## Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Domicile certificate (or 15-year-old ration card, voter ID, school leaving certificate or birth certificate)
- Income certificate (not needed if the family has a yellow or orange ration card)
- Aadhaar-linked bank passbook
- Applicant's photograph
- Marriage certificate (for women married into Maharashtra from another state)

## How to apply
1. Apply online at ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in, or offline through the Anganwadi worker, ward office or Setu Suvidha Kendra.
2. Fill in the form, upload documents and submit; complete Aadhaar eKYC.
3. After approval, monthly payments start by DBT.

## Helpline
181 (women's helpline).
