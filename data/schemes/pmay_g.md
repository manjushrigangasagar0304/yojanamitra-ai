# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

Ministry: Ministry of Rural Development
Type: Centrally sponsored rural housing scheme, launched November 2016, extended to 2028-29
Official: https://pmayg.nic.in | https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmay-g

## Objective
Provide a pucca house with basic amenities to every rural family that is homeless or lives in a kutcha or dilapidated house.

## Benefits
- One-time assistance of Rs 1.20 lakh per house in plain areas.
- Rs 1.30 lakh per house in North Eastern states, hill states (Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand) and the UTs of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh, and in difficult / IAP districts.
- Up to Rs 12,000 extra for a toilet through Swachh Bharat Mission - Gramin.
- 90-95 days of unskilled wage work under MGNREGA for building the house.
- Minimum house size 25 sq m including a cooking area.
- Paid by DBT in instalments linked to construction stages.
- Cost is shared between the Centre and states at 60:40 in plain areas and 90:10 in NE and Himalayan states.

## Eligibility
- Rural household that is houseless, or lives in a kutcha house with 0, 1 or 2 rooms with kutcha walls and kutcha roof.
- Beneficiaries are identified from SECC-2011 housing deprivation data and the Awaas+ survey, verified by the Gram Sabha.
- Households owning a pucca house are not eligible.

## Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account details (Aadhaar-linked)
- MGNREGA job card
- Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM) registration number
- Photo of the current kutcha house (where asked)

## How to apply
1. Eligible families are listed through the Awaas+ survey; contact the Gram Panchayat or Block Development Office to get included.
2. After Gram Sabha verification, the house is sanctioned.
3. Track status and beneficiary list on pmayg.nic.in using the registration number.
