# Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)

Ministry: Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises; implemented by KVIC, State KVIBs and District Industries Centres
Type: Credit-linked subsidy scheme
Official: https://www.kviconline.gov.in/pmegpeportal | https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmegp

## Objective
Create self-employment by helping people set up new micro-enterprises in the non-farm sector.

## Benefits
- Margin money subsidy on a bank loan for a new unit.
- Maximum project cost: Rs 50 lakh for manufacturing, Rs 20 lakh for business/service.
- The bank lends the balance as a term loan plus working capital.

| Beneficiary category | Own contribution | Subsidy (urban) | Subsidy (rural) |
|---|---|---|---|
| General | 10% | 15% | 25% |
| Special (SC/ST/OBC/minorities/women/ex-servicemen/disabled/transgender/NER/hill and border areas) | 5% | 25% | 35% |

- An area recorded as a village in revenue records counts as rural, whatever its population.
- Upgradation of an existing PMEGP/MUDRA unit: 15% subsidy (20% in NER and hill states), up to Rs 1 crore project.

## Eligibility
- Any individual above 18 years of age.
- No income ceiling.
- For projects above Rs 10 lakh (manufacturing) or Rs 5 lakh (business/service), the applicant must have passed class 8.
- Only new projects qualify. Units that already got a government subsidy under another scheme are not eligible.
- Self-help groups, charitable trusts, societies and production cooperatives can also apply.

## Documents required
- Aadhaar card
- Detailed project report
- Caste / special category certificate (if claiming special rate)
- Educational qualification certificate
- Rural area certificate (if the unit is in a rural area)
- Bank account details

## How to apply
1. Register on the PMEGP e-portal and fill in the online application with the project report.
2. The application is scrutinised by the District Task Force Committee and sent to a bank.
3. After the bank sanctions the loan, the applicant completes mandatory Entrepreneurship Development Programme (EDP) training.
4. The margin money subsidy is kept in the bank for 3 years and then adjusted against the loan.
