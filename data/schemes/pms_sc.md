# Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste Students (PMS-SC)

Ministry: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment; implemented by state governments
Type: Centrally sponsored scholarship
Official: National Scholarship Portal https://scholarships.gov.in; in Maharashtra, MahaDBT https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in

## Objective
Help Scheduled Caste students complete education after class 10, from class 11 up to PhD, so they can move to higher education.

## Benefits
- Reimbursement of compulsory non-refundable fees charged by the institution (tuition, exam and other fixed fees), subject to state fee-fixation rules.
- A yearly academic allowance, which depends on the course group (for example professional degrees get more than general graduate courses) and on whether the student is a day scholar or hosteller.
- Extra support for students with disabilities.
- Money is paid through DBT into the student's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

## Eligibility
- Student must belong to a Scheduled Caste.
- Parents'/guardians' annual income from all sources must not exceed Rs 2.5 lakh.
- Must be studying a recognised post-matric or post-secondary course (class 11, 12, diploma, ITI, graduation, post-graduation, professional courses, PhD) in a recognised institution.
- Students repeating the same class are generally not funded again.
- Note: the government has proposed raising the income limit to Rs 4.5 lakh. Check the National Scholarship Portal or MahaDBT for the limit in force this year.

## Documents required
- Caste certificate
- Income certificate of parents/guardian
- Marksheet of the last qualifying exam
- Admission letter and fee receipt
- Aadhaar card and Aadhaar-linked bank account
- Domicile certificate

## How to apply
1. Register on the National Scholarship Portal or the state scholarship portal (MahaDBT in Maharashtra).
2. Fill in the application, upload documents and submit before the portal deadline (usually between October and December each academic year).
3. The institute verifies the application, then the district / state welfare office approves it.
4. Renew the scholarship every year on the same portal.
